# SPIRALFM1 licensing

SPIRALFM1 is an independent firmware built on Mycelium 1.0 (Mandalars), SLOOP (isod89) and
Felucca (hugelton). It retains Felucca's and SLOOP's GPL-3.0-only code licence and upstream
notices (full text in `LICENSE`; third-party licence texts collected in `LICENSES/`).

## Original SPIRALFM1 artwork and icons

The current `assets/icons.png` contains 86 original geometric pixel drawings, generated from scratch by `tools/gen_spiralfm1_icons.py`. No upstream atlas pixels, external images or font glyphs are used to draw these firmware symbols. The replacement atlas and generator are distributed under GPL-3.0-only. Exact names and atlas hash are recorded in `assets/SPIRALFM1-ICONS.json`; asset terms are in `assets/SPIRALFM1-ICONS-LICENSE.txt`.

The spiral mark and wordmark in `assets/spiralfm1/` are drawn by `tools/gen_spiralfm1_svg.py` from
geometric primitives (logarithmic spirals, arcs and nodes); no upstream logo path, external picture
or font outline is an input. The splash bitmap in `build/gen/spiralfm1_logo.h` is a rasterisation of
that artwork. Mycelium's own brand and logo remain Mandalars' and are not used here.

The upstream icon atlas was replaced and is not the current atlas. The separate web-editor Fukiai font remains MIT, as credited below. The SPIRALFM1 procedural drums (`tools/gen_spiral_drums.py`, provenance in `assets/spiralfm1/samples/`) have no external audio input; the legacy CC0 sample bank remains credited below.

Upstream reference material and the Hügelton Sample Pack generated by `tools/gen_waves.py` retain their original Copyright (C) 2026 Hügelton Instruments, all rights reserved, with asset terms to be published by their owner. These are not the replacement SPIRALFM1 icons or drums. SLOOP does not build the SLICE demo loop generated by that tool.
